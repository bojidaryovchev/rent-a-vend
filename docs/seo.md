# Search: measured demand and what was built on it

Measured 2 August 2026 via DataForSEO, `location_code 2100` / `language_code bg`
— Bulgaria, Bulgarian. Live Google SERPs, volumes, difficulty, CPC and
competitor backlink profiles. 457 keywords harvested across five seed
expansions.

Volumes are Google-Ads-derived, so close variants are grouped and totals across
near-identical phrasings carry double-counting risk. "No data" means below the
reporting threshold (~10/mo), not literally zero.

---

## 0. The three facts that govern everything

1. **The rental market is roughly 300 searches a month.** SEO cannot carry this
   business. The site must convert what little arrives, exceptionally well.
2. **Difficulty is 0–8 and the site ranking #1 has 35 backlinks.** Ranking is
   trivial. The constraint is demand, not competition — which inverts the usual
   priority order.
3. **The demand that exists is operator-shaped** — permits, tax, profitability,
   ~710/mo at KD 0 — while the product is workplace-shaped and the rental
   cluster is ~300/mo.

~300/month across every phrasing found, and the three кафе- variants may be

At a generous 30% click-through from position 1 and 10% enquiry conversion,
300/mo is about **nine enquiries a month**. The counterweight is the CPCs:
€1.07–1.33 on the coffee-rental terms against €0.15–0.31 on the generic ones.
Advertisers pay real money for exactly the visitor this site wants.

## 2. The category and regulatory clusters

The head term is ten times the rental market and only slightly harder:

| Keyword | Vol | KD | Intent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **вендинг машини** | **2,900** | 7 | transactional |
| вендинг машина за кафе | 720 | 8 | transactional |
| **вендинг машина цена** | **260** | **4** | commercial |
| вендинг машина за храна | 260 | 0 | transactional |
| вендинг автомати | 260 | 0 | commercial |
| вендинг машини пловдив | 70 | **41** | navigational |

`вендинг машини` is mostly *purchase* intent, and sales live on `buy-a-vend`,
so this site cannot serve it directly. `/naem-ili-pokupka` is what converts
"should I buy?" into "rent instead" — it is the only page here that monetises
the biggest term in the market, and no vending specialist ranks for the
rent-versus-buy question in either index (Google returns a business blog and a
news site; Bing returned an article about cherry pickers).

Note `вендинг машини пловдив` at **KD 41**, by far the hardest term measured,
because vending-plovdiv.com holds a knowledge panel and Google reviews there.
City terms are not uniformly easy.

The regulatory cluster is the largest body of demand in the market:

~710/month, most of it at KD 0. This is operator intent — people who want to

lead cluster, and it is the only way to build topical authority in this niche at
all.

## 3. Model names carry no traffic

All 21 catalogue model names were submitted — Concerto, Canto, Kikko, Snakky,
Astro, Opera, Brio, Colibri, Solista, Korinto, Kalea, Maestro, Sfera, Tango,
Melodia, Festival, Samba, Wittenborg 9100, FAS Easy 6000, Crane Merchant and the
rest. **Not one returned measurable volume**, and across 457 harvested keywords
no model name appears. Competitors do target them; nobody searches them.

Model pages therefore stay — they are the catalogue, the conversion surface, the
enquiry landing point and the citable unit for AI search — but **they are not a
traffic strategy**, and the model names are kept for accuracy rather than for
search ([decisions.md](decisions.md) D23a, D24a).

This finding is Bulgarian and does not generalise: in German, `necta automaten`
is 90/mo at KD 0 and seven German dealers rank on specific model names. That is
`buy-a-vend`'s problem, not this site's.

## 4. Intent collision on the head term

**`вендинг машини под наем` means two opposite things and both rank.** Some
searchers want to rent a machine. Others own premises and want an operator to
place one — vending-plovdiv's meta description offers *"10% от оборота на
машината или твърд наем"*, boss-vending runs *"Търсим места под наем"*, and
alo.bg segments "Места вендинг автомати" (36 ads) separately from "Вендинг
автомати наем" (16 ads).

Consequences: head-term traffic is part-poisoned and will convert below
expectation — that is not a page failure — and nobody has built the page that
separates the two, which is a cheap win. The home page names who the site is for
in the first screen and routes the other audience away rather than trying to
convert it.

## 5. The live SERP

`вендинг машини под наем`: vending-bg.com #1 (with a dedicated
`/vending-mashini-pod-naem` URL) · **a Facebook post at #2** · olx.bg ·
bulvending · vendingzone · madeforarcade (arcade machines — intent bleed) ·
bazar.bg · vendingpartners · **Facebook again at #10** · vending-plovdiv at #17
and alo.bg at #18, both space-rental offers. Plus a knowledge panel and Google
reviews for vending-plovdiv.com.

The field is weak enough that social posts out-rank most vending companies.

**AI Overviews appeared on 4 of 6 queries tested** — the permit query, the
placement-requirements query, the profitability query and `вендинг машина цена`
— all in the regulatory and commercial clusters, none on the navigational ones.
Content built there competes for citation, not only for clicks.

## 6. Links do not gate this market

| Domain | Domain rank | Backlinks | Referring domains | Spam score |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **vending-bg.com** — *ranks #1* | **11** | **35** | **33** | **55** |
| vendingpartnersbg.com | 38 | 1,359 | 35 | 26 |
| bulvending.com | 26 | 184 | 48 | 1 |
| **breaktime.bg** | 25 | 172 | **70** | 1 |
| cityvending.bg | 11 | 46 | 20 | 22 |

The site ranking #1 for the money term has 35 backlinks and domain rank 11.
vendingpartners' 1,359 backlinks are an illusion: 710 come from one blogspot and
423 from one coffee site — 83% from two domains, i.e. sitewide footer links.

**30–50 quality referring domains reaches parity with the market leader.** For a
brand starting from zero authority that is the single most useful fact in this
document.

There is a visible playbook and an anti-playbook. **breaktime.bg** has the
strongest profile — 70 referring domains, spam score 1, built from real Bulgarian
press (capital.bg, bnr.bg, nova.bg, actualno.com, offnews.bg, frognews.bg) — and
it ranks #1 on both regulatory queries. **bulvending** built its profile from a
coordinated set of niche directories (tatkovci.bg, vetworld.bg,
interiordesigners.bg, proektanti.bg, architects.bg), nearly all first seen
January–July 2025. That is a purchased network and it is not what to copy.

## 7. The architecture that was built on this

```
/                             home — conversion hub, and the intent split
├── commercial
│   ├── /kafe-mashini-pod-naem/            coffee category
│   ├── /snak-mashini-pod-naem/            snack category
│   ├── /kombinirani-mashini-pod-naem/     combination category
│   ├── /avtomati-za-studeni-napitki/      cold drinks category
│   │     └── /<model-slug>                48 model pages
│   ├── /naem-ili-pokupka                  rent vs buy — the highest-leverage page
│   └── /tseni                             the price explainer
├── conversion
│   ├── /koya-mashina    recommender      /zapitvane   enquiry form
│   ├── /kak-raboti      how it works     /kazusi      case studies
│   └── /za-nas · /kontakti · /vaprosi
├── authority
│   └── /rakovodstva/
│       ├── razreshitelno-za-vending-mashini   210/mo  KD 0
│       ├── iziskvaniya-za-postavyane           90/mo  KD 0
│       └── registratsiya-babh                 ~30/mo  KD 0
└── legal  /obshti-usloviya · /poveritelnost · /biskvitki · /usloviya-za-naem
```

Slugs are Latin transliterations of Bulgarian, matching the client's own
convention. `dynamicParams = false`, so only real slugs render and everything
else 404s.

**Three pages carry the weight, in this order:** `/naem-ili-pokupka`, because it
is the only page that monetises the 2,900/mo term; the home page, because it
carries the largest rental term and has to resolve the intent collision; and
`/tseni`, because publishing a real price against a field that publishes none is
the entire positioning, on the one term where people ask for it.

**Venue pages were deliberately not built.** The taxonomy supports
`/vending-mashini-za-ofis` and eleven siblings and it is tempting, but every
venue term tested returned no data. Twelve near-duplicate pages against zero
measured demand is textbook thin content. Venue targeting stays where it works:
as filters, as the recommender's input, and as text on model pages. Revisit only
if Search Console shows real impressions.

**The fourth guide was also not built.** `dogovor-za-postavyane` is 10/mo at
KD 38 — the worst ratio in the cluster — and the contract mechanics it would
cover are already on `/usloviya-za-naem`.

## 8. What ships in the head

- **Canonical on every page.** `pageMetadata()` takes the path as a required
  argument, so a page cannot be added without one.
- **Titles kept under 60 characters including the brand suffix**, with an escape
  hatch: `pageMetadata({ brandSuffix: false })` drops the suffix where the
  computed title already carries the keyword and a price. Five competitors
  truncate in the SERP.
- **Structured data:** `Organization` / `LocalBusiness` with opening hours and a
  contact point, `WebSite`, `Product` with an `Offer` carrying
  `businessFunction: LeaseOut` and a `UnitPriceSpecification` in `MON`,
  `BreadcrumbList`, `ItemList` on categories, `Article` and `FAQPage` on guides.
  **No `AggregateRating` and no `Review`** — there are none, and fake review
  markup is a manual-action risk on top of being a lie.
- **`og:image` on every page** — model pages use their machine photograph,
  everything else falls back to the logo.
- **Indexing is gated** on `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_INDEXABLE`. Until it is exactly
  `true`, `robots.txt` disallows everything and `/llms.txt` 404s. It must not be
  flipped before real prices land: a catalogue cached at derived figures teaches
  search engines numbers that are slow to correct.
- **`/zapitvane` is `noindex, follow`** rather than disallowed in robots.txt —
  disallowing blocks crawling, not indexing. The admin carries an unconditional
  `noindex`.
- **`/llms.txt`** is generated from the live catalogue and gated on the same
  flag. GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot are not blocked; citation is the
  point.
- **`lastmod` is deliberately omitted** from the sitemap. No per-page content
  date is tracked, so the only available value is build time, which would assert
  that every URL changed on every deploy. Google stops trusting a `lastmod` it
  proves inaccurate.
- **CSP is deliberately absent.** A working policy has to allow Plausible,
  Turnstile and the Google Maps embed plus inline JSON-LD and Next's bootstrap;
  a wrong clause fails silently and takes the enquiry form with it. It belongs
  behind a browser test on a deployed preview, not guessed at in a config file.

## 9. Measurement

Primary metric: **enquiries per month, and whether each carries machine and term
context**. Not traffic.

The authority layer is measured on rankings, referring domains and AI citations
— judging a permit guide by enquiries would kill content that is working as
intended. And if `вендинг машини под наем` traffic converts far below the rest,
that is the intent collision showing up in the data, not a broken page.

Connect Google Search Console at launch. It replaces every estimate in this
document with first-party truth.
